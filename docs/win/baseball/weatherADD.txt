dew_point_temperature      # air density / carry context
air_pressure_at_sea_level  # air density / carry context
symbol_code                # condition label / rain context

wind_speed + wind_from_direction
Affects carry. Wind out helps fly balls. Wind in suppresses carry. Crosswind can matter less directly.

air_temperature
Warmer air is less dense, so the ball generally carries better. Colder air suppresses carry.

relative_humidity / dew_point_temperature
Humidity and dew point affect air density and ball carry. Dew point is often cleaner than humidity because humidity depends on temperature.

precipitation_amount / symbol_code
Rain risk can delay games, affect pitcher grip, field conditions, and scoring environment.

air_pressure_at_sea_level
Pressure affects air density. Lower pressure generally helps carry; higher pressure suppresses carry.
