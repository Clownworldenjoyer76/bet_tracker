Edge dot scale (e0 → e5)

e0 — nearly invisible white (no edge)
e1 — muted gray
e2 — slate blue-gray
e3 — yellow (--picks-yellow)
e4 — orange (#ff7043)
e5 — red with glow (#ff2d2d) — highest edge

The intentional split is: green = your edge/value, blue = the actual bet, yellow = time/metadata. That way your eye goes bet → edge in two distinct colors without competing.
